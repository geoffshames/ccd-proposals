## 7. Strategic opportunity
The festival's documented pain is the journey in and out (heat, exits, remote zones, surge, "where's my ride"). The audience is already primed to solve it socially (Reddit threads, shuttle guides). An approved Uber partnership converts the worst moment of the day into a branded, useful, shareable one. Paid media's job: make the partnership known BEFORE arrival, attach it to a code, and make the ride itself feel like the first act.
